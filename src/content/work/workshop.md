built: "The action layer on top of the company brain. A brand-book rules gate, 7 channel adapters, a performance poller and a spend guard turn market intel into on-brand content for Meta ads and organic, LinkedIn, Google Ads, blog, Resend email and WhatsApp."
situation: "The Intel Hub had already filled the company brain with competitor ads, customer language from Reddit and Facebook groups, and keyword gaps. But intel that nobody acts on is a cost, not an asset. A 2-person team could not brief, design, write and traffic creatives for 7 channels by hand, and a pre-launch consumer app had no budget for an agency or a stack of creative tools. The usual shortcut, generic AI content, was off the table: off-brand creatives waste ad spend and damage the brand at the same time."
task: "As Founder's Associate I had to turn that intel into on-brand content across paid and organic, make the brand book the law for every asset, and give the team a clear call on which creatives to boost and which to stop, all for less than €50 a month in API costs."
action:
  - "Built Workshop as the action layer on top of the company brain. It clusters real customer pain from Reddit, Facebook groups and reviews with competitor and SEO signals into scored content opportunities, so every brief starts from demand and not from a blank page."
  - "Turned the brand book into a rules gate. Every draft passes coded checks first and an AI reviewer second, before a human sees it, and the founder's always-no rules are written straight into the generator."
  - "Generated hyper-realistic, on-brand creatives and copy with the OpenAI API and Higgsfield, with ElevenLabs for voice. One opportunity becomes an ad creative, an organic post, a blog article and an email, each cut to the format of its channel."
  - "Built 7 channel adapters: Meta ads, Meta organic (Instagram and Facebook), LinkedIn, Google Ads, the blog CMS, Resend email and WhatsApp. Drafts land in one review inbox, approvals run through Slack and WhatsApp, and every paid ad is created paused in Meta Ads Manager and Google Ads, so no budget goes live until a person switches it on."
  - "Added a performance poller that reads impressions, clicks, CTR, CPC, CPM, spend, reach and frequency back from each channel at 24 hours, 48 hours, 7 days and 30 days, with GA4 and Google Search Console covering the blog and organic side."
  - "Scored every creative against its channel baseline, not against gut feel: 1.3% CTR for Meta ads, 3.9% for Google Ads and 1.8% for Meta organic. A creative at 1.2x its baseline or better is flagged to boost, for example a Meta ad at 1.6% CTR against 1.3%. A creative at 0.8x or lower is flagged to stop, and one whose CTR falls to 60% of its first reading is marked as creative fatigue."
  - "Closed the loop. Hooks, angles and themes that beat their baseline get a bigger share of the next batch, and saturated patterns are rotated out, so spend moves to the creatives most likely to lift ROAS and away from the ones that push CPC up. A spend guard caps daily and monthly generation, so API costs cannot run away."
result: "One system that turns market intel into hyper-realistic, on-brand content for 7 channels, for less than €50 a month in API costs and at about 40% lower AI spend per creative than the earlier tool setup. Performance and Analytics show which creatives to boost on Meta ads and which to stop, so the budget follows CTR and CPC evidence and not opinion."
